What the agent can, and what it can't.

The guarantee isn't a better prompt. The money tools simply have no field for a price, a payee or a budget.

The agent can

  • search 361 products in 9 categories
  • read the missions you signed
  • propose a basket of SKUs and quantities
  • place an order the gateway approved, once
  • verify the audit chain

The agent cannot

  • state a price or an amount
  • choose who gets paid
  • create a mission or raise a budget
  • reuse an approval
  • read the signing key

get_productprice, attributes, compatibility, return policy, stock left
