# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in SELLABLE to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including in disagreement;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- owning mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances;
- trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission;
- anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
**harshimp123455@gmail.com**. Every report will be reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected. The maintainer may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
harmful.

Responses scale with the problem: a private warning, then a temporary ban
from interaction, then a permanent ban for repeated or severe violations.
